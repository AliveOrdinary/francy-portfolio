    body: >-
      Milton Malayalees is a community association for Malayalees living in
      Milton, Ontario. They wanted an identity that represents both Kerala and
      Milton, and asked for two things from home: an elephant, and the
      *nettipattam*, the golden caparison worn by elephants at Kerala's temple
      festivals.
