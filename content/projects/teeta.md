    label: 01 — The name
    heading: A common factor, and something to eat
    body: >-
      Teeta was named together with the client. In maths, theta (θ) is a
      common factor, the thing that connects everything else. In Malayalam,
      തീറ്റ (theeta) is an informal word for eating. Together they make a name
      that’s playful, local and easy to remember.
